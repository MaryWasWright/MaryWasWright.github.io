About this project

A home for Pyr knowledge that does not wander off.

Public site for people who want history in one sitting, a catalog of good writing already on the internet, and practical pages on guardian work, health, and companion life.

How the catalog works

Each entry is a title, source, year if known, topic tag, original summary, and outbound link. We do not reprint full articles.

New sources go into data/articles.json. Search and filters update themselves.
