Health · coat · structure

A mountain coat on a deep-chested giant.

Pyrs are often sturdier than many modern breeds. That is not the same as indestructible.

Not veterinary advice. Restless after a meal, unproductive retching, or sudden lameness is same-day vet territory.

Bloat / GDV

  • Two or three smaller meals instead of one trough.
  • No hard running right after eating.
  • Skip the raised bowl unless a vet has a reason.
  • Ask about gastropexy when the dog is already under for a spay or neuter.

Joints, growth, and staying lean

Hip and elbow dysplasia show up in large breeds. Keep them lean. The double coat hides weight. You should feel ribs with light pressure. Unexplained lameness in a mature Pyr is not “just old” until a vet has said so.

Double dewclaws

Hind double dewclaws are a breed feature. Serious Pyr people treat removal as mutilation unless a vet is dealing with a real injury. Check them when you groom.

Coat, heat, and Texas

  • Brush weekly; daily when the undercoat blows.
  • Do not shave for summer. The coat insulates against heat as well as cold.
  • Shade, untippable water, no midday heroics on hot sand.
  • Look under the coat. Skin problems hide there.
